# Apex Global Technologies — Commercial Pricing, Sales Playbook & Discount Matrix

## 1. Commercial Product Editions & Pricing Structure
Apex Global licenses its cloud platform through annual and multi-year subscription tiers:

### 1. Team Edition
- **Target Customer**: Early-stage companies and teams with up to 25 active users.
- **Base Pricing**: **$49 per active user / month** (billed annually at $588/user/year).
- **Core Inclusions**: Standard Silver support (9am-5pm), shared cloud tenant, up to 500,000 monthly API calls, standard integrations (Slack, Google Workspace).

### 2. Business Edition
- **Target Customer**: Mid-market enterprises (25 to 250 active users).
- **Base Pricing**: **$89 per active user / month** (billed annually at $1,068/user/year).
- **Core Inclusions**: Enterprise Gold support, dedicated database read-replicas, SSO / SAML authentication, up to 5,000,000 monthly API calls, audit log exports.

### 3. Enterprise Platinum Edition
- **Target Customer**: Large corporations and regulated enterprises requiring bespoke infrastructure and compliance.
- **Base Pricing**: **Custom annual contract starting at $35,000 / year**, plus volume user licensing.
- **Core Inclusions**: Enterprise Platinum 24/7 support with 15-minute P1 SLA, isolated single-tenant VPC deployment, custom data retention policies, HIPAA/SOC 2 BAA agreements, and a Dedicated Technical Account Manager (TAM).

## 2. Sales Discounting Authority Matrix
To safeguard gross margins and maintain pricing integrity, all commercial discounts from published list prices require documented approval in Salesforce prior to formal quotation:

| Discount Percentage | Required Approval Level | Required Business Justification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0% – 10%** | Account Executive (AE) Discretion | Multi-user bundle or upfront annual commitment |
| **10.1% – 20%** | Regional Sales Director | Multi-year agreement (2+ years) or competitive takeaway |
| **20.1% – 30%** | Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) | Strategic enterprise logo or high-volume commitment |
| **> 30%** | Chief Financial Officer (CFO) | Exceptional enterprise partnership; requires margin impact analysis |

---

## 3. Standard Payment & Contract Terms
- **Standard Invoicing Terms**: **Net 30 days** from invoice date via ACH or wire transfer.
- **Extended Terms (Net 45 / Net 60)**: Require written approval from Finance.
- **Contract Duration**: Standard contracts are executed on a **12-month minimum commitment**. Multi-year agreements (24 or 36 months) are eligible for an automatic 5% annual price protection guarantee.
- **Termination for Convenience**: Enterprise contracts do not permit termination for convenience mid-term. Early termination for material breach requires a 30-day cure period.
