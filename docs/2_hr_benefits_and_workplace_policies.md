# Apex Global Technologies — HR Policies, Benefits & Workplace Guidelines

## 1. Working Models & Core Working Hours
Apex Global embraces a flexible hybrid and remote-first workplace model:
- **Core Hours**: All employees, regardless of location, are expected to be available for synchronous communication between **10:00 AM and 4:00 PM in their local time zone**.
- **Home Office Setup Stipend**: Full-time employees receive a one-time, tax-free **$750 home office allowance** within their first 60 days to purchase ergonomic desk chairs, monitors, or peripherals.
- **Monthly Internet & Connectivity Stipend**: Employees working remotely receive a **$75 monthly reimbursement** on their regular paycheck to offset broadband costs.

---

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Guidelines
- **Annual PTO Allocation**: Full-time team members accrue **25 days of paid time off per calendar year**, accrued at approximately 2.08 days per month worked.
- **PTO Rollover Limit**: A maximum of **5 accrued, unused PTO days** can be rolled over into the following calendar year. Any additional unused PTO beyond 5 days expires on December 31st.
- **Sick & Wellness Days**: In addition to PTO, employees are allocated **10 paid sick/mental health days annually**. A doctor’s note is only required for consecutive absences exceeding 3 business days.
- **Company Holidays**: Apex observes 11 standard public holidays per region plus a company-wide year-end recharge week between Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

---

## 3. Parental & Family Leave Policy
- **Primary Caregiver Leave**: 16 weeks of 100% fully paid leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
- **Secondary Caregiver Leave**: 8 weeks of 100% fully paid leave.
- **Eligibility**: Employees become eligible for parental leave after completing **90 days of continuous service** with the company.
- **Phased Return-to-Work**: Returning parents may opt to work 80% hours (4 days/week) at 100% pay during their first 4 weeks back to ease the transition.

---

## 4. Healthcare, Retirement & Total Rewards
- **Health, Dental & Vision Insurance**: Apex covers **90% of the employee premium** and **75% of dependent premiums** across high-deductible (HDHP with HSA) and PPO healthcare tiers.
- **Health Savings Account (HSA) Match**: For employees enrolled in HDHP, Apex contributes an annual match of **$1,000 for individual coverage** or **$2,000 for family coverage**.
- **401(k) / Pension Match**: Apex provides a **100% matching contribution up to 4% of base salary**. Employee contributions and company match are immediately 100% vested from day one.
- **Professional Development Budget**: Every full-time employee receives an annual learning stipend of **$1,500** for approved books, conferences, certifications, and courses.
- **Wellness Allowance**: Up to **$60 per month** reimbursed for gym memberships, fitness trackers, yoga classes, or mental health counseling apps.
