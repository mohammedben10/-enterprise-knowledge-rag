# Apex Global Technologies — Customer Success & Service Level Agreements (SLA)

## 1. Overview & Service Availability Commitment
Apex Global commits to maintaining a **99.9% Monthly Uptime SLA** across our software platforms and hosted API services, calculated 24 hours per day, 7 days per week, excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least 72 hours in advance.

---

## 2. Customer Support Tiers & Target Response Times

| Customer Tier | Coverage Window | Priority 1 (Critical) | Priority 2 (Major) | Priority 3 (Standard) | Priority 4 (Low) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enterprise Platinum** | 24/7/365 | **<= 15 Minutes** | <= 1 Hour | <= 4 Hours | <= 12 Hours |
| **Enterprise Gold** | 24/5 (Mon-Fri) | **<= 1 Hour** | <= 4 Hours | <= 8 Hours | <= 24 Hours |
| **Standard Silver** | 9am - 5pm Local | **<= 4 Hours** | <= 8 Hours | <= 24 Hours | <= 48 Hours |

### Incident Priority Definitions
- **Priority 1 (Critical Outage)**: Production platform completely unavailable or critical customer-facing transactions blocked with no workaround.
- **Priority 2 (Major Impairment)**: Core feature degraded or substantial system performance slowdown affecting a majority of end users; temporary workaround available.
- **Priority 3 (Standard Bug / Request)**: Minor functional defect, administrative dashboard error, or non-critical issue with operational workaround.
- **Priority 4 (Low / General Inquiry)**: General product how-to questions, feature requests, or minor cosmetic inconsistencies.

---

## 3. SLA Breach Remedies & Service Credits
If monthly platform availability falls below guaranteed thresholds, eligible Enterprise customers are entitled to contractual service credits applied to their subsequent billing invoice:

| Monthly Availability Percentage | Service Credit Percentage |
| :--- | :--- |
| **99.0% – 99.89%** | **10% credit** of monthly fee |
| **95.0% – 98.99%** | **25% credit** of monthly fee |
| **< 95.0%** | **50% credit** of monthly fee |

To claim a service credit, customer accounts must submit a written request via their designated Customer Success Manager (CSM) within **30 days** of the month in which the incident occurred.

---

## 4. Customer Escalation Pathway
When an incident is unresolved within targeted resolution windows, the following escalation stages trigger automatically:
1. **Tier 1 (Support Engineer)**: Initial ticket triage and troubleshooting.
2. **Tier 2 (+1 Hour from breach)**: Escalated to Senior Solutions Architect and On-Call Engineering Lead.
3. **Tier 3 (+2 Hours from breach)**: Escalated to VP of Customer Success and Director of Site Reliability Engineering (SRE).
4. **Tier 4 (+4 Hours from breach)**: Executive briefing delivered by Chief Technology Officer (CTO) with hourly status page updates.
