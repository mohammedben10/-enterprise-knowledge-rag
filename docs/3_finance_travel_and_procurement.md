# Apex Global Technologies — Corporate Travel, Expenses & Procurement Policy

## 1. Corporate Travel Guidelines
Business travel must always be pre-approved by the employee’s direct manager prior to booking flights, lodging, or transport.

### Flight Class Rules
- **Domestic Flights & Short-Haul (< 6 Hours)**: All employees must book standard Economy or Main Cabin seating. Preferred seating (extra legroom) is permissible if the price difference is under $50.
- **Long-Haul & International Flights (>= 6 Hours)**: Employees traveling continuously across time zones for 6 or more hours are eligible to book **Premium Economy or Business Class** with Director approval to ensure readiness for client meetings.

### Hotel & Lodging Standards
- Maximum lodging rate is **$275 per night** in tier-1 metropolitan cities (e.g., New York, London, San Francisco, Tokyo) and **$190 per night** in all other regions.
- Taxes and mandatory resort fees are covered outside this limit. Booking through corporate portal Navan / Egencia is required.

---

## 2. Meal Allowances & Daily Per Diem
Meals during authorized business travel are reimbursed on a per-diem basis or via itemized receipts:
- **Domestic Per Diem**: **$85 per day** ($20 breakfast, $25 lunch, $40 dinner).
- **International Per Diem**: **$120 per day** ($25 breakfast, $35 lunch, $60 dinner).
- Alcohol is eligible for reimbursement only during formal client entertaining dinners, up to two drinks per attendee, provided an itemized receipt and list of attendees are attached.
- Solo personal alcohol purchases while traveling are strictly non-reimbursable.

---

## 3. Expense Submission & Reimbursement Deadlines
- All business expenses must be submitted through the corporate expense software (Ramp) within **30 calendar days** of the transaction date.
- Itemized receipts are required for any individual charge equal to or exceeding **$25.00 USD**. Credit card statements without itemized merchant receipts are not valid audit documentation.
- Approved expenses are processed on the subsequent bi-weekly payroll cycle via direct deposit.

---

## 4. Corporate Procurement & Purchasing Approval Matrix
All hardware, software, subscriptions, consulting services, and contracts must adhere to the following sign-off thresholds:

| Purchase Amount (USD) | Required Approvers | SLA for Approval |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$0 – $1,000** | Direct Manager | 1 Business Day |
| **$1,001 – $10,000** | Department Head / Director | 2 Business Days |
| **$10,001 – $50,000** | VP of Department + Head of Finance | 3 Business Days |
| **$50,001+** | Chief Financial Officer (CFO) + Legal Procurement | 5 Business Days |

- **Software Subscriptions**: Any new SaaS software tool, regardless of cost (even free tiers), must undergo an initial IT security and data privacy assessment before company data is uploaded.
