# Apex Global Technologies — Company Overview & Organizational Directory

## 1. Company Mission & Profile
Apex Global Technologies is an enterprise B2B software and cloud analytics solutions provider founded in 2020. Our mission is to empower global businesses with resilient, AI-powered operational workflows and actionable data infrastructure. We operate on three fundamental principles:
- **Customer First**: Every technical and operational decision is measured against client success and trust.
- **Radical Candor & Transparency**: We share context broadly, debate constructively, and communicate with absolute clarity.
- **Continuous Learning & Excellence**: We foster high agency, rapid iteration, and high standards in engineering and customer delivery.

## 4. Key Communication Channels & Directory
For seamless cross-functional collaboration, team members should use designated Slack channels:
- `#general`: Company-wide announcements and town hall updates.
- `#ask-hr`: Confidential benefits, payroll questions, and workplace policy inquiries.
- `#ask-finance`: Procurement requests, expense reimbursement questions, and invoicing.
- `#it-helpdesk`: Hardware provisioning, software licenses, VPN, and Okta access issues.
- `#it-security-urgent`: 24/7 security incident reporting (monitored by SecOps on-call).
- `#client-escalations`: Priority P1/P2 customer support alerts and executive escalation briefings.
